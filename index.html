AirDraw Pro: AI-Powered Contactless Spatial Air Drawing Web Application

AirDraw Pro is a state-of-the-art touchless air drawing system that tracks 21 3D hand landmarks in real time through webcams and phone cameras using Google MediaPipe Hands, client-side WebAssembly, and dual-pole 1-Euro adaptive jitter stabilization.

Key Capabilities & Performance Standards

  • Single-Finger Drawing Invariant: The index fingertip (☝️) creates sub-pixel smoothed neon strokes at 60 frames per second.
  • Adaptive 1-Euro Filter: Low-frequency cutoff f_min=1.2Hz and beta=0.025 eliminate hand tremor while preserving zero-latency response during quick strokes.
  • Mobile Phone Architecture: Supports both front selfie camera and rear environment camera switching with auto-mirroring adaptation.
  • Spatial In-Air Controls: Pinch-to-click (🤏) and dwell timer (250ms) enable hands-free UI button selection and floating window dragging.
  • Comprehensive Multi-Finger Gestures: Open palm eraser (🖐️), three-finger Undo (3️⃣), four-finger Redo (4️⃣), rock clear (🤟), and thumbs up save (👍).
  • Complete Client-Side Privacy: 100% on-device WebAssembly inference. No video feeds or biometric telemetry are ever uploaded or transmitted across networks.

Mobile Smartphone & Tablet Compatibility

AirDraw Pro is engineered for smartphones (iOS Safari iPhone, Android Chrome, iPad, Samsung Internet). It features Screen Wake Lock to keep the display active during mid-air drawing, proactive AudioContext unlocking on touch, and lightweight neural network inference options for 60 FPS mobile performance.
